Privacy & Safety

GPS is used only while tracking or exploring and is not sent to a server. This app does not provide medical advice. Use your own judgement, adapt challenges, hydrate normally, and allow recovery.

PWA offline mode requires installation from a secure HTTPS host. GPS requires browser location permission and may not work offline on every device.
